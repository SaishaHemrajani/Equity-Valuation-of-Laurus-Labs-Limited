# Equity-Valuation-of-Laurus-Labs-Limited
Equity valuation of Laurus Labs Limited using DCF-FCFF, Relative Valuation, and Economic Profit, supported by five-year financial forecasts and a valuation football field. 

**Valuation date:** 31 March 2026  
**Company:** Laurus Labs Limited  
**Sector:** Pharmaceuticals  
**Project type:** Individual academic project

## Methodology

### 1. Historical Financial Analysis
- Collected and analysed five years of historical financial statements.
- Examined revenue, profitability, operating performance and financial position.
- Reviewed management earnings conference calls to understand business drivers, growth opportunities and risks.

### 2. Financial Forecasting
- Developed 5-year financial projections.
- Incorporated revenue growth, operating margins, capital expenditure, working capital and terminal growth assumptions.
- Considered alternative forecasting scenarios.

### 3. Valuation Methods
- **DCF-FCFF:** Estimated intrinsic value using forecast free cash flows to the firm and terminal value.
- **Relative Valuation:** Benchmarked Laurus Labs against comparable pharmaceutical companies using P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples.
- **Economic Profit:** Evaluated value creation relative to the required return on invested capital.

### 4. Valuation Synthesis
Developed a football field chart to compare valuation ranges, point estimates and the valuation-date market price. Sensitivity analysis was used to examine the effect of WACC and terminal growth assumptions.

## Key Findings

The model's equal-weighted reference valuation was approximately ₹304.52 per share, compared with a valuation-date market price of ₹992.70 per share.

Under the assumptions and methodology used, the analysis indicated substantial overvaluation as of the valuation date and produced a **SELL** conclusion for the academic exercise.

These figures are historical model outputs and should not be interpreted as a current price target or investment recommendation.
